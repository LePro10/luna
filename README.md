# Luna

**A personal AI that runs my life. Built at 16.**

Luna is my own AI assistant — not a chatbot in a browser tab, but one agent connected to everything I use every day:

- **Phone** — reads the screen, taps, types and opens apps for me (banking apps are blocked)
- **Calls** — I can call Luna like a person and talk through my day
- **Screen time** — app limits and focus blocks that Luna enforces on my phone
- **School & learning** — study sessions built from my own notes, planned around my tests
- **Mail, calendar, plans, files, notes** — searched and connected without me telling her where to look
- **Browser** — Luna can work in her own Chrome while I watch

Self-hosted on my own server, with an Android app. Built over four months with AI coding agents — I direct them, they write the code.
