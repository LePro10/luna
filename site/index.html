One agent

Everything I use,
connected to one AI.

I don't tell Luna where to look. I say what I want, and she finds the context herself.

  • She uses my phone

    Reads the screen, taps, types, opens apps. A stop button stays visible. Banking apps are blocked.

  • I can call her

    A real call, like with a person. She also replaces the phone's assistant: hold the side button.

  • She blocks TikTok

    App limits, focus blocks and lock times, enforced on my phone.

  • She gets me ready for tests

    Short practice sessions from my own notes, around my weak spots, planned before the test.

  • She sorts my inbox

    Ads to the trash, important mail into events and tasks. Before anything is sent, she asks.

  • She works on the web

    Her own Chrome: research, forms, clicking through sites while I watch live.

How it's built

AI agents write the code.
I direct them.

  1. 1

    I describe it

    What Luna should do, in plain words, and what "done" means.

  2. 2

    Agents build in parallel

    Several coding sessions at once, each with its own part of the code.

  3. 3

    An AI tests Luna

    A second AI pretends to be me and checks what Luna actually did, not what she said.

  4. 4

    Every push goes live

    Self-hosted on my own server, with an Android app.

Ground rules

An AI with access to my life
needs rules.

  • Asks before it's irreversible. Sending mail or messages needs my yes.
  • Everything can be undone. Changes to plans, notes and calendar come with undo.
  • Proof before claims. "Done" only counts once she has checked that it worked.
  • Hands off the dangerous stuff. No banking, password or 2FA apps.
