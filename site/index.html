One agent

Everything I use,
connected to one AI.

I don't tell Luna where to look. I say what I want, and she finds the context herself: across my calendar, mail, notes, files and school.

  • Phone

    She uses my phone for me.

    Luna reads the screen, taps, types and opens apps. A stop button stays visible the whole time. Banking apps are blocked.

  • Voice

    I can call her.

    A real call, like with a person. She replaces the phone's built-in assistant: hold the side button and she's there.

  • Screen time

    She blocks TikTok.

    App limits, focus blocks and lock times that Luna enforces on my phone, so I actually study instead of scrolling.

  • School

    She gets me ready for tests.

    I drop in my notes. She builds short practice sessions around my weak spots and plans them before the test date.

  • Mail & calendar

    She sorts my inbox.

    Ads go to the trash, important mail turns into events and tasks. Before anything leaves my account, she asks.

  • Browser

    She works on the web.

    Luna has her own Chrome. She researches, fills in forms and clicks through sites while I watch live.

How it's built

AI agents write the code.
I direct them.

  1. 1

    I describe it

    What Luna should do, in plain words, and what "done" means.

  2. 2

    Agents build in parallel

    Several AI coding sessions at once, each with its own part of the code, so they don't collide.

  3. 3

    An AI tests Luna

    A second AI pretends to be me, talks to Luna and checks what she actually did, not what she said.

  4. 4

    Every push goes live

    Self-hosted on my own server, with an Android app. A finished change deploys itself.

Ground rules

An AI with access to my life
needs rules.

  • Asks before it's irreversible. Sending mail or messages needs my yes.
  • Everything can be undone. Changes to my plans, notes and calendar come with undo.
  • Proof before claims. "Done" only counts once she has checked that it worked.
  • Hands off the dangerous stuff. No banking, password or 2FA apps.
